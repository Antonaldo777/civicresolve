## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the standard for modern civic infrastructure: authoritative, responsive, transparent, and radically accessible. It serves residents, field technicians, and municipal leaders navigating public service workflows, emergency response, and community resolutions.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Authoritative & Legitimate:** Grounded in institutional integrity without bureaucratic inertia.
- **Empathetic & Approachable:** Clear public-facing communication free of convoluted administrative jargon.
- **Real-Time & Telemetric:** Crisp telemetry signals, clear status states, and dynamic live spatial intelligence.
- **Accountable & Algorithmic:** Visible, transparent AI governance represented by precise indicator badges and verified automated actions.

### Design Aesthetic
The aesthetic bridges **Modern Civic Utilitarianism** and **Precision SaaS**. It leverages deep sovereign navies paired with high-contrast slate canvases, clean functional borders, subtle tactile status indicators, and balanced typographic weight. Interfaces prioritize high scan-speed, dependable spatial context, and actionable certainty during routine inquiries and critical civic emergencies.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional authority with high-visibility utility signaling. All combinations meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA standards (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy; 3:1 for large graphical elements).

### Core Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0F172A`):** Used for primary municipal branding, high-priority navigation bars, critical CTA buttons, and dominant headline hierarchy. Conveys stability, governance, and resolve.
- **Secondary Civic Cyan (`#0284C7`):** Directs active interactive workflows, portal hyperlinks, active tabs, and primary map coordinate markers.
- **Tertiary Civic Emerald (`#0D9488`):** Signifies validated civic infrastructure, completed work orders, environmental programs, and resolved public records.
- **Neutral Slate (`#64748B`):** Anchors secondary data labels, borders, icon fills, and structural scaffolding against crisp `#F8FAFC` base canvases.

### Functional Alerts & Telemetry
- **Emergency Rose (`#E11D48`):** Reserved for immediate public hazards, 911 dispatch escalation, and critical municipal alerts.
- **Warning Amber (`#F97316`):** Identifies impending deadlines, maintenance backlog warnings, and infrastructure traffic advisories.
- **AI Sparkle Accent (`#38BDF8` to `#818CF8`):** Reserved exclusively for algorithmic summaries, automated intake routing, and verified machine-assisted civic decisions.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural and expressive architectural moments with **Inter** for dense transactional reading, ticket tracking, and multi-language public documentation.

### Type Pairings
- **Display & Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural balance and civic gravitas without feeling cold or detached. Its geometric clarity maintains legibility on municipal dashboards, field tablet displays, and large public displays.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Inter is tuned for high-density tabular reads, status rosters, and form-heavy municipal records. Tabular numbers (`tnum`) must be enforced for ticket IDs, telemetry counters, and GIS coordinate feeds.
- **Responsive Handling:** Desktop headings scale down smoothly on viewports narrower than `768px` using specified mobile overrides to preserve screen real estate and prevent unformatted wrap-around headers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8pt architectural rhythm structured around a fluid, bounded grid. The spatial system accommodates operations center monitors, desktop browsers, and handheld field inspection devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and bounded 40px outer margins. Supports side-by-side geographic map panes, telemetry lists, and detail inspectors.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Geographic maps collapse to anchored split drawers or responsive tabs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px safe margins. Stacks operational cards vertically with persistent bottom-anchored actions for one-handed operation.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Component padding and inner margins rely strictly on `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (40px).
- Internal element gaps within cards, forms, and status clusters prioritize tight vertical associations (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to preserve contextual linkability.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a **crisp planar surface hierarchy** supported by subtle ambient shadows and low-contrast slate outlines (`#E2E8F0` on light canvases). It rejects heavy skeuomorphism in favor of structural clarity.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground layer (`#F8FAFC`). Flat, unshadowed background for layout canvases and application frames.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow (`0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`). Used for service cards, civic lists, and static reports.
3. **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Items):** Slightly elevated with an extended diffusion shadow (`0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`). Used for interactive resolution cards and focal dispatch rows.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays, Flyouts & Map Callouts):** Floating map inspectors, civic popovers, and modal alert sheets (`0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`). Always accompanied by a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) for contrast against satellite or spatial map vectors.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** geometric profile. Components balance human approachability with structural discipline.

### Geometric Application
- **Standard Controls & Cards:** Base corner radius is `0.5rem` (8px), offering a sharp, engineered appearance for data-heavy civic interfaces.
- **Containers & Map Overlays:** Cards, dialogs, and map panels utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to cleanly separate structural layers.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Badges, telemetry chips, and filter tags use full circular/pill borders (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish dynamic metadata and tags from interactive square buttons and input fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Civic Action:** Filled `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height 40px (desktop), 48px (touch/mobile). Rounded 8px (`0.5rem`). Hover transitions to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover introduces `#F1F5F9`.
- **Emergency Action:** Filled `#E11D48` with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#BE123C`. Used strictly for dispatches, evacuations, and critical service escalations.

### Status Indicators & Telemetry Badges
- **Shape:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with a height of 24px, inner padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **States:**
  - *Active / Healthy:* `#CCFBF1` background, `#0F766E` text, featuring a pulsating 6px `#0D9488` telemetry dot.
  - *In Review / In Progress:* `#E0F2FE` background, `#0369A1` text, 6px static `#0284C7` dot.
  - *Warning / Delinquent:* `#FFEDD5` background, `#C2410C` text, 6px static `#F97316` dot.
  - *Emergency:* `#FFE4E6` background, `#BE123C` text, featuring an animated pinging 6px `#E11D48` dot.

### AI Resolution Badge (Gemini Sparkle Accent)
- Used when automated categorization, field dispatch recommendation, or summary transcription is active.
- **Styling:** Linear gradient border (`1px`) transitioning from `#38BDF8` to `#818CF8`. Background is translucent `#F0F9FF` (80% opacity) with backdrop blur.
- **Iconography:** A four-point sparkle glyph positioned leading the label `label-sm` with a dual-tone fill (`#0284C7`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** White background, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), 8px radius, height 40px. Focus ring is an explicit 2px `#0284C7` offset with 2px transparent white buffer. Error state swaps border and focus ring to `#E11D48`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px size. Checkboxes have a 4px radius; radios are circular. Active state fills with `#0F172A` with a white checkmark or center pip.

### Map-Integrated Card Surfaces
- **Structure:** Modular surface positioned on top of spatial map viewports.
- **Header:** Contains ticket ID (`telemetry-mono`), current status pill, and timestamp.
- **Content:** Headline (`title-md`), short summary, location address with pin icon, and estimated resolution timeline.
- **Footer:** Direct action triggers (e.g., "Dispatch Crew", "Verify Resolution", "Route Navigation"). Backed by high-density white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and Level 3 ambient shadow.
